RU <-> EN Translator (laptop version) - fully offline

1. Double-click FieldTranslator.exe
2. Click the big colored bar to choose the direction (Russian->English or English->Russian)
3. Click LISTEN (or press Space) and talk. Text appears live; the translation
   appears under each phrase when the speaker pauses.

Keys: Space = start/stop listening, Tab = switch language.
Keep the "models" folder next to FieldTranslator.exe. The whole folder can be
copied to a USB stick and run from there on any Windows 10/11 PC.
No internet is ever used.
